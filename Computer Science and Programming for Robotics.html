What is computer science and programming robotics?

Robots process sensor data, perform cognition and plan actions using computer programs that are executed on a processor. Computer programs are essentially a set of instructions that operate on an input to produce an output

What is computer Sci and robotic?

Computer Science is the study of computers and computational systems. It includes topics such as programming, algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and software development. CS is essential for developing software, managing data, and solving complex problems using technology.

Robotics is a branch of engineering and computer science that deals with designing, building, and programming robots. It combines mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and computer science to create machines that can perform tasks autonomously or with human guidance. Robotics is used in industries like manufacturing, healthcare, space exploration, and artificial intelligence.

About computer science and programming robotics

Robotics is an interdisciplinary field at the crossroads of engineering and computer science involving the design and operation of robots. A robot is a machine that is capable of performing complex tasks either automatically or assisted by humans.Robotics is a branch of engineering and computer science that involves the conception, design, manufacture and operation of robots. The objective of the robotics field is to create intelligent machines that can assist humans in a variety of ways.

Computer science and programming are fundamental to robotics, the field of designing, building, and operating robots. Robotics is an interdisciplinary field that combines computer science with engineering.
